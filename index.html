I am a Quantum Fellow at EPCC, University of Edinburgh. My research connects quantum many-body physics, tensor networks, and quantum information.

I develop tensor-network methods to study complex quantum systems and understand the possibilities and limits of quantum simulation.

Research

My work explores how quantum systems evolve, how their complexity can be described, and what this means for quantum computation. My interests include:

  • Tensor networks. Developing efficient descriptions of quantum states and dynamics, with links to machine learning and classical simulation.
  • Quantum dynamics. Studying thermalisation, quantum chaos and many-body scars, including the structures that allow long-lived coherent behaviour.
  • Quantum information. Understanding entanglement and quantum magic, and how these resources help characterise the limits of classical simulation and the potential for quantum advantage.
